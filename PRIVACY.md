# SkipShorts privacy policy

SkipShorts does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

- The only thing the extension stores is your on/off settings, using Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync`.
  If you're signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs these settings between your own devices.
  They are never sent anywhere else.
- The extension runs only on youtube.com and facebook.com. It reads those pages in your browser to decide
  what to hide, and hides it. Nothing it reads leaves your browser.
- It makes no network requests of its own, loads no remote code, and contains no analytics or tracking.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/gazillion101/skipshorts/issues

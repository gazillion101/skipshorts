## Store listing tab

**Category:** Productivity. **Language:** English.

**Description:**

SkipShorts removes YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels from your feeds, so you can watch what you came for
without getting pulled into endless short-video scrolling.

YouTube
• Hides Shorts shelves on the home page, subscriptions, search results and the watch-page sidebar
• Hides individual Shorts mixed into video grids and lists
• Hides the Shorts button in the sidebar, the Shorts tab on channel pages and the "Shorts" search filter
• Optional: opens any youtube.com/shorts/ link in the normal video player

Facebook
• Hides Reels shelves, reel posts and the Reels shortcuts
• Hides sponsored posts and the "Sponsored" box in the right column
• Hides suggested posts from pages and groups you don't follow (the ones with a Follow or Join button)
• Hides the "People you may know" and "Your group suggestions" carousels
• Hides posts carrying Meta's "AI content" label

Every option can be switched on or off from the toolbar popup, and changes apply immediately.

Private by design: no tracking, no ads, no data collection. The extension only stores your on/off settings.
Free and open source: https://github.com/gazillion101/skipshorts

Note: Facebook labels are matched in English, so the Facebook options work when Facebook is set to English.

**Homepage URL:** https://github.com/gazillion101/skipshorts
**Support URL:** https://github.com/gazillion101/skipshorts/issues

## Privacy tab

**Single purpose:**
Hides short-form video (YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels) and unwanted feed content such as ads and suggested
posts on YouTube and Facebook.

**storage justification:**
Saves the user's on/off choices for each option in the popup.

**Host permission justification (youtube.com, facebook.com):**
The content scripts run on these two sites only, to find Shorts, Reels, ads and suggested posts on the page
and hide them. No page data is read for any other purpose or sent anywhere.

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code.

**Data usage:** check none of the data types; tick all three certifications.

**Privacy policy URL:** https://github.com/gazillion101/skipshorts/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
